**LPC Services prepares your U Visa application.** This is a protection designed for people who were **victims of a crime inside the United States** and suffered physical or mental abuse as a result.

## Police certification is the decisive step

The requirement that holds the whole file together is **cooperation with law enforcement** and the certification that proves it (Form I-918B), signed by the agency that investigated the crime. Without that document the case does not move forward, and obtaining it is usually the slowest part of the process. That is why we start working on it from day one, in parallel with the rest of the file.

## What this service includes

- Police certification management (Form I-918B)
- Collection of medical reports and records
- Organization of evidence of harm suffered
- Personal statement preparation
- Complete Form I-918 packet assembly
- Certified translations when needed

## Who is this for?

This service is for **crime victims** who have suffered substantial physical or mental abuse and have been helpful or are likely to be helpful in the investigation or prosecution of criminal activity.

LPC Services prepares and organizes the documentation; we are not a law firm and we do not provide legal advice.
