**LPC Services helps you prepare your affirmative asylum application.** This is the application filed voluntarily and directly with USCIS, not before a judge. We organize your personal statement, gather supporting evidence, and translate the necessary documents so your file arrives complete.

## The one-year deadline

The law sets a **one-year limit from your arrival in the United States** to file the application. Filing after that deadline, without a properly documented exception, can cost the case. That is why the first thing we review with you are the dates: when you entered, with what document, and what evidence supports it.

## Why the way your statement is written matters

The declaration must be **concise, chronological, and transparent**, and it must connect your story to the points the law requires. A disorganized, contradictory, or dozens-of-pages statement with no clear link to persecution raises immediate doubts for the officer and puts the case at risk. Our job is to make your story clear and verifiable, with no embellishment and no gaps.

## What this service includes

- Review of entry dates and the one-year deadline
- Drafting and structuring your personal asylum declaration
- Reviewing your story to identify key persecution points
- Collecting and organizing documentary evidence
- Certified translations of relevant documents
- Complete Form I-589 packet assembly
- Final consistency review before submission

## Who is this for?

For people who **entered the United States with inspection** (with a visa, ESTA, or parole), **have no open case in immigration court**, and are seeking protection based on a well-founded fear of persecution in their home country.

If you already have a case in immigration court, the applicable process is [defensive asylum](/en/services/defensive-asylum).

LPC Services prepares and organizes the documentation. We are not a law firm, we do not provide legal advice, and we do not assess which immigration route applies to your case — that is determined by the licensed attorney.
