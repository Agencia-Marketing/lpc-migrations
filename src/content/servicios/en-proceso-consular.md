**LPC Services prepares and organizes your consular processing file.** This is generally the route for people who **entered without inspection** (through irregular routes or the border) and must complete their residence process at the consulate in their home country.

## How it works

Unlike [adjustment of status](/en/servicios/ajuste-de-estatus), which is resolved inside the United States, consular processing usually involves two steps that must be prepared well in advance: **applying for a legal waiver** and then **leaving the country** to attend the consular appointment, before being able to re-enter legally.

Leaving the country is a serious step with no quick way back. That is why the file must be complete and free of inconsistencies before anyone travels. Whether this route applies to your case, and when, is assessed by the licensed attorney.

## What this service includes

- Completion and review of Form DS-260
- Organization of the NVC file and follow-up on requested documents
- Assembly of the supporting documentation for the waiver application
- Preparation of the affidavit of support and its financial evidence
- Collection of civil records, police certificates, and passports
- Certified translations of home-country documents
- Coordination of the medical exam and interview documentation
- Final consistency review before the interview

## Who is this for?

For people with an approved petition who must complete residence processing at the consulate in their country: those already outside the United States, and those who entered without inspection and cannot process residence from inside the country.

The visa decision rests solely with the consulate. LPC Services prepares and organizes the documentation; we are not a law firm and we do not provide legal advice.
