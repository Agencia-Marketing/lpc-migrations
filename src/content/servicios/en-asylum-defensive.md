**LPC Services helps you prepare the documentation for your defensive asylum application.** We organize your personal statement, gather supporting evidence, and translate the necessary documents so your file reaches the immigration court complete and well organized.

### What this service includes

- Drafting and structuring your personal asylum declaration
- Reviewing your story to identify key persecution points
- Collecting and organizing documentary and country-conditions evidence
- Certified translations of relevant documents
- Complete Form I-589 packet assembly
- File organization aligned with your court dates
- Final consistency review before submission

### Who is this for?

This service is for people who **entered the United States without inspection** and **already have an open case in immigration court**. Unlike affirmative asylum, which is filed with USCIS, here the application is filed within the proceedings before the judge.

Representation before the court is handled by the licensed attorney assigned to your case. LPC Services prepares and organizes the documentation; we are not a law firm and we do not provide legal advice.
